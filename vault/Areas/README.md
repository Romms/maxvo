# Areas

Ongoing responsibilities with no finish line — a standard to maintain, not a goal to complete (PARA's
"Areas," distinct from `vault/Projects/`'s time-bound commitments). Reviewed via a dormancy check at
the weekly `checkin` — "still being maintained? anything changed?" — not against a deadline.

| Area | Що означає "в нормі" | Востаннє перевірено | Деталі |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health | Тиск у межах норми, немає симптомів, яким немає пояснення | 2026-08-03 | [[Health/README\|Health]] |

Each Area gets its own folder (`vault/Areas/<Назва>/`) with at least a `README.md` — same
subfolder-index convention as `vault/Projects/` and `vault/Daily/` (see `CLAUDE.md` Architecture).
Unlike a Project's detail file (opt-in, only for something complex), the folder here is the default:
an Area is designed to keep collecting relevant material for as long as it's relevant, so there's
always room to grow without a later file→folder migration.
